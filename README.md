# Moraen

**THE ICE IS GONE. THE TROLLS ARE STIRRING.**

When the great ice came, the dwarves sealed their treasure halls and fled. Ten
thousand years later the ice is gone, the halls lie buried in the moraine, and
you're the youngest of the clan, sent back to bring the treasure home. Dig
through the rubble, gather the gems to open the old doors, and mind the
boulders, because some of them blink: the stone trolls are thawing, and awake
they stomp about hurling stones. Twenty caves and five intermissions, five
difficulty levels, a faithful engine for the 1984 cave-digging classic's rules,
and trolls of our own.

## Play

With [av](https://asciiverse.sh), the Asciiverse client:

```
av install moraen
av play moraen
```
